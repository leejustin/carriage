# App Review notes (paste into App Store Connect)

Carriage is a distraction-free writing app that turns iPhone into a small typewriter.

**iPhone Duo.** Half-fold the phone like a laptop: the top half shows the sheet of paper and the bottom half becomes a full typewriter keyboard. Held like a book (fold down the middle), the page shows as a two-page reading spread with a note to turn it on its side to type; typing is always paper on top, keys below. Close it and the outer screen shows the page for reading and light editing with the standard keyboard. Folding never restarts the app; the page, the carriage position and any unsaved typing carry straight across.

**Regular iPhone.** Carriage is made for iPhone Duo; on other iPhones it runs as a fully working "pocket" machine, and a one-time note on first launch says so. Paper on top, a smaller typewriter keyboard below. With a hardware keyboard attached, the paper goes full screen and the on-screen keys hide; sounds and the carriage animation still play.

**The keyboard is drawn by the app.** The round keys are not the system keyboard. Autocorrect is off on purpose: it's a typewriter. For dictation, other languages or assistive input, Settings › Accessibility › "Use the system keyboard" swaps in the standard iOS keyboard. Every key has a VoiceOver label.

**Writing focus.** The moon button clears the screen down to the page and keys (tap the paper to see the controls). Carriage also offers a Focus Filter: add it to a Focus in Settings › Focus › Focus Filters and Carriage clears itself whenever that Focus is on. Carriage doesn't change Focus or notification settings itself; it shows the user how to.

**Also in the app:** notebooks and a daily journal page; version history and Markdown copies in Files (iCloud Drive › Carriage); import of text files; print and share-as-image; widgets and Siri shortcuts ("Start writing in Carriage"); a night desk; a two-page reading spread when the Duo is held like a book.

**Modes.** Typewriter mode is the default. Correcting mode works like a normal editor (backspace works). Typewriter mode has no delete: the X-OUT key types an x over the last character, and exports leave struck text out (plain text) or show it as ~~strikethrough~~ (Markdown).

**Purchase (non-consumable, no subscriptions, no ads):** Unlock Carriage (`com.carriage.app.unlock`). Every paper and ribbon and the Italian portable machine sound are included in the free version, which is the first 5,000 words written; the unlock adds unlimited words and three more machine sounds (Settings › Machine sound plays a sample of each). Past that, the writer finishes the line, then the unlock letter appears and the keys rest until the unlock (reading, exporting and the library stay free). To see the letter without typing 5,000 words: Settings › Purchases › Unlock Carriage. Restore Purchases is in Settings and on the unlock screen.

**Sounds** are real typewriter recordings released CC0 (public domain) on Freesound; sources in docs/sounds/SOURCES.md.

**Privacy.** No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Pages sync through the user's own iCloud private database.

No sign-in is needed to review the app.
