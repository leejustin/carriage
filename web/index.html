Why Carriage

It started with my parents' old typewriter.

I found it and fed a sheet of paper in, and something changed. I couldn't type fast: every key needed a real press. When the bell rang I had to decide where the line ended and push the carriage back by hand. And I couldn't take anything back. Every letter went onto the paper for good, so a mistake stayed there, struck through with x's.

So I slowed down. I thought before I pressed a key. I wrote less, and meant more of it.

Our phones are built for the opposite. So I rebuilt that typewriter for the iPhone Duo, the most advanced phone I own, and made it the most analog thing it can be.

Made for iPhone Duo

Half-fold it like a laptop.

  • The page stands on the top screen; a full typewriter keyboard sits on the bottom.
  • Hold it like a book and your draft becomes a two-page spread to read.
  • Fold it shut mid-sentence and your page is saved; open it and you're back where you were.
  • Set it up once and your phone goes quiet whenever Carriage is open.

Also runs on any iPhone, with the page and keys sharing one screen. It's at its best half-folded on a Duo.
