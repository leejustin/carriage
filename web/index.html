Why Carriage

It started with my parents' old typewriter.

I fed a sheet of paper in and started typing, and noticed I was slowing down. Each key took a real press. When the bell rang near the margin, I decided where the line ended and pushed the carriage back myself. A typo stayed on the page, struck through, so I chose my words before I pressed them.

I was thinking about every sentence, and I was enjoying it.

Our phones are built for creating and consuming more than our brains can take in. I wanted one place on mine that asks for less: a page, a line at a time, and the sound of the keys. So I rebuilt that typewriter for the iPhone Duo.

Made for iPhone Duo

Half-fold it like a laptop.

  • The page stands on the top screen; a full typewriter keyboard sits on the bottom.
  • Hold it like a book and your draft becomes a two-page spread to read.
  • Fold it shut mid-sentence and your page is saved; open it and you're back where you were.
  • Set it up once and your phone goes quiet whenever Carriage is open.

Also runs on any iPhone, with the page and keys sharing one screen. It's at its best half-folded on a Duo.
